The hive-slot sample locations and red-channel contrast criterion in
src/game/detection/hive_slots.cpp derive from nm_detectHiveSlots at that checkout.

instead of assigning occupancy from individual pixels. The camera survey
in src/automation/hive_survey.cpp uses bounded zoom/pitch normalization
and two distinct half-turn views. Candidates do not establish ownership
or player position. The optional claim workflow in the same file uses the
six slotMove distances, nm_spawnMoveTo movement directions and the
nm_detectHiveSlots walking-view restoration. A fresh claim prompt and a
separately confirmed owned-hive result are required before success.
